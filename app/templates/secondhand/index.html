How it works

  1. Buy - from a person (no invoice: your own purchase voucher, with their ID proof) or from a dealer (their invoice). No input tax credit is ever taken on these goods.
  2. Used stock - every unit with what you paid, when, and from whom.
  3. Sell - pick the unit (IMEI), enter the price: you see the margin and its GST before you save. A margin-scheme invoice (MSI) is made; GST is never added on top.
  4. e-Way bills - for goods going out to a buyer or coming in from a seller{% if not eway_on %} (switch on the e-Way Bill module to make them here){% endif %}.
  5. Register & returns - the margin register; GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B pick the sales up on their own.
